03 · Work

Four small apps, each built from a real problem.

scholartrack.base44.app

01 · Live app · Built with Base44

ScholarTrack

Centralized student workspace consolidating semester modules, deadlines, and reference links into one clutter-free dashboard.

Problem
School tasks, lessons, and materials were scattered across notes and chat threads.
Built
A student dashboard that keeps tasks, lessons, and materials in one organized view.
Learned
The hardest part wasn't the app itself, it was deciding how to group tasks, lessons, and materials so the dashboard didn't feel cluttered.

bizflow-core.base44.app

02 · Live app · Built with Base44

BizFlow Pro

End-to-end business operations tracker connecting raw material costs, stock levels, and sales records in one place.

Problem
Product costs, materials, inventory, and sales records lived in separate sheets.
Built
A business workspace that organizes costs, materials, inventory, and sales together.
Learned
I learned to map the whole cost-to-sale workflow on paper first, since skipping that step would have meant rebuilding the screens halfway through.

A learning companion for Gregg shorthand — progressive lessons, stroke drills, and a searchable outline dictionary.

Building
A learning app with lessons, exercises, handwriting practice, and a digital dictionary.
